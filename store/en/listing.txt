[Full description]
Hop on the back of the world's most laid-back capybara. Your tiny rider grabs a gatling gun and a rocket pod, and the zombie horde never stands a chance!

CapyZombie is a first-person 3D zombie shooter with a cute low-poly look. Play a quick run or go all-in on endless mode. Collect mandarins to power up, take down bosses, and climb the global leaderboard.

■ HOW YOU FIGHT
• Drag the left side to move and the right side to aim — your gatling gun fires nonstop.
• Rocket Pod, Stomp, Decoy, the Barrage ultimate and the B2 Mandarin Bombing: one tap sends zombies flying.
• Push the stick all the way to charge and bowl zombies over like pins.
• Touch aim assist makes hitting targets easy on a phone.

■ 6 BATTLEFIELDS, 72 ZOMBIES, 18 BOSSES
Yuzu Farm, Neon City, Desert Ruins, Candy Park, Snowy Onsen and Moonlit Graves — each with 12 zombie types of its own. Shield bearers, healers, splitters, chargers, toxic zombies that leave poison puddles… choosing who to shoot first is the whole game.
A boss appears every minute. Defeat all three to clear the battlefield — including ranged bosses that rain snipes, mortars and bullet hell.

■ GROW STRONGER WITH MANDARINS
• Pick one of 28 upgrade cards every time you level up.
• Level up two upgrades together to unlock 12 weapon fusions: Storm Gatling, Sun Mandarin, Missile Storm, Capybara Tank and more.
• Clear battlefields to unlock late-game gear like the laser, jetpack and power suit.

■ 8 RIDERS
Duck, frog, cat, dog, hamster, rabbit, penguin and raccoon. Each rider has its own perk, and you can dress them up with hats, face gear and neckwear.

■ 156 ACHIEVEMENTS, 61 TITLES, GLOBAL LEADERBOARD
Every achievement has bronze, silver, gold and platinum medals. Earn titles from medals and leaderboard ranks, show them before your nickname, and compete on each battlefield's global leaderboard.

■ 4 DIFFICULTIES
From Easy to Extreme. Harder modes give up to 2.5× leaderboard points.

■ ALSO
• Free, no ads
• English, Korean, Japanese and Simplified Chinese
• Choose your graphics quality for smooth play on any phone
